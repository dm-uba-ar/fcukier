Housing

In principle the housing reservations will be arranged by the organizers. If you have special housing needs or preferences please let us know. If you prefer to make your own housing arrangements please keep us informed. More details on reservations will be sent by email when available, as soon as possible.


HOUSING FOR STUDENTS
The participating students have reservations in shared rooms at University Housing on-campus. These rooms are located in Ciudad Universitaria, near "Campo de Deportes" (Sports field), across the street from Pabellón 3 (School of Architecture). See a map here.
